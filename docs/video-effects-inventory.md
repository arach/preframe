## Top 30 Effects for Demo Video & UI Presentations

Criteria for ranking: subordinate to UI (doesn't compete), loopable, achievable in post, high perceived production value.

---

### Group 1 — The 10 Most Promising
*Highest impact-to-effort ratio for UI/demo contexts. Start here.*

| # | Effect | Why it works for UI demos | Difficulty |
|---|--------|--------------------------|------------|
| 1 | **Dark Mode Palette Shift + Neon Accent Glow** | The native aesthetic of modern dev/SaaS tools. UI sits naturally on deep blacks; selective neon accents frame without competing. | Low |
| 2 | **Holographic Iridescence** | Slow rainbow sheen over neutral footage (glass, water, concrete). Extremely premium, mesmerizing, zero competition with UI elements. | Low |
| 3 | **Product Halo Glow** | Radial soft glow from center of frame — the background literally defers to whatever sits in front of it. Built for this exact use case. | Low |
| 4 | **Parallax Depth Separation** | Depth planes moving at slightly different speeds. The UI appears to float in real 3D space without any green screen. | Medium |
| 5 | **Fluid Simulation Tint** | Video hues bleed and swirl as if in liquid ink. Alive but fully abstract — never pulls focus. Looks extremely high-end. | Medium |
| 6 | **Tilt-Shift Miniaturization** | City or environment looks like a toy model. Selective focus band keeps midground sharp; everything else softly blurs. Always gets comments. | Low |
| 7 | **Particle System (source-sampled)** | Footage dissolves into a constellation of moving colored particles. Abstract enough to never fight UI but still warm and human. | Medium |
| 8 | **Reaction-Diffusion / Turing Patterns** | Organic biological spots and stripes evolve slowly over footage. Rare in product video — instantly signals deep tech or AI. | Medium |
| 9 | **Flow Field / Optical Flow Streaks** | Real motion vectors from the footage visualized as ink-like streaking lines. Data-viz aesthetic — perfect for analytics or infra products. | Medium |
| 10 | **Depth-Map Selective Tint (MiDaS)** | AI-estimated depth drives per-layer color grading — foreground one tone, background another, no green screen. UI layers feel compositionally correct. | Medium |
