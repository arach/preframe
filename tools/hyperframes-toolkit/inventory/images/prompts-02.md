

# Abstract Data Visualization Prompts — Flowing Particles

---

**Prompt 1 — "Data Stream Nebula"**
```
Abstract data visualization rendered as an infinite stream of luminous particles flowing through
dark space, forming a celestial nebula shape from flowing data points, dark background (#0a0a0f),
single accent color (#4ade80) throughout, cinematic volumetric lighting with soft god rays,
particles vary in size and opacity creating depth, 16:9 cinematic composition, hyper-detailed
rendering, 8K resolution, ethereal atmosphere
```

---

**Prompt 2 — "Neural Network Storm"**
```
Abstract data visualization as a turbulent storm of flowing particles resembling neural network
connections, dark background (#080810), single accent color (#4ade80), cinematic rim lighting
highlighting particle edges, particles weave and spiral in organic flow patterns, 16:9
composition with dramatic negative space, subsurface scattering on particle clusters, moody
atmosphere, photorealistic digital art
```

---

**Prompt 3 — "Chromatic Flow Field"**
```
Abstract data visualization as a dynamic flow field composed of millions of glowing particles
moving in cohesive waves, dark background (#0d0d12), single accent color (#4ade80), cinematic
key light from upper left casting soft shadows, particle trails create motion blur effect,
16:9 widescreen composition, depth of field with bokeh particles in foreground, atmospheric
fog layer, cinematic color grading
```

---

**Prompt 4 — "Quantum Data Constellation"**
```
Abstract data visualization transformed into an ethereal constellation of quantum particles,
dark background (#060610), single accent color (#4ade80) with subtle luminosity variations,
cinematic backlighting creating glowing halos around each particle, particles cluster and
disperse in rhythm like breathing data, 16:9 panoramic composition, starfield depth layers,
dreamlike quality, volumetric god rays piercing through particle streams
```

---

**Prompt 5 — "Liquid Architecture"**
```
Abstract data visualization as flowing liquid architecture made of suspended particles,
dark background (#0a0a0e), single accent color (#4ade80), dramatic cinematic lighting with
high contrast chiaroscuro, particles form arching structures that appear frozen mid-flow,
16:9 composition with particles concentrated in center third, reflective particle surfaces,
soft ambient occlusion between particle clusters, cinematic still life aesthetic
```

---

Each prompt emphasizes the flowing-particle concept while varying the mood—from cosmic to neural to architectural—while maintaining the dark backdrop, single green accent, and cinematic lighting throughout.
