

**1. Title Card**

*Ghost Protocol* — Digital decay at the edge of the city. Layered signal noise, fractured drum patterns that stutter and reset like corrupted surveillance feeds. Somewhere between transmission and memory.

**2. Atmospheric Description**

The fluorescent hum of Corridor 7 bleeds into distorted 808 kicks—each hit a door slamming in an empty station. Filter sweeps cut through frequencies like flickering monitors at 3am. Ghost Protocol: the signal that was never meant to be received.

**3. Cinematic Write-up**

A Korean subway at 3am—the platform lights flicker in arrhythmic pulses. Beneath the silence, glitch percussion scratches against concrete: broken beats, dead clicks, phantom footsteps. Surveillance cameras watch empty benches. And somewhere, a frequency pulses that shouldn't exist. This is **Ghost Protocol**: the sound of systems running after everyone's gone home.
