

---

**[Verse 1]**
The city wakes in perfect lines,
neon veins and concrete spines,
every step measured, every turn known,
the grid hums soft, a mechanical tone.

Cameras blink at crosswalk pace,
faces blur beneath the same gray face,
we count the blocks from A to Z,
tracing the seams of symmetry.

---

**[Chorus]**
But somewhere in the seam,
where the concrete meets the dream,
a crack holds light—
small, broken, barely bright.

---

**[Verse 2]**
The satellites read license plates,
the alleys whisper binary fates,
yet in the gap between the walls,
a stranger laughs, a vendor calls.

Not data. Not code. Not designed.
A coffee cup, a scribbled sign,
fingers tracing something true
the grid was built to overwrite.

---

**[Chorus]**
So here we are, the unaligned,
the static in the perfect sign—
small, broken, barely bright,
we are the crack that holds the light.
